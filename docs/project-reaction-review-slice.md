# Project reaction identity and independent human review

Local candidate following 678a627. Google Drive remains the PDF original store. No live migration, OAuth activation or deployment is included.

The project reaction page links from project and paper research navigation. A user creates a stable project-scoped identity from a current case revision, then explicitly maps every compound to that identity. Mapping checks structural context, formula, charge, stereochemistry, protonation, participant roles and coefficients, reaction representation and claim scope. Names and aliases do not establish identity. Unknown attributes remain unknown; the required human attestation and reason assert equivalence within this bounded model, not automated chemical identity recognition. Definition corrections require a new identity and explicit mapping revisions, retaining prior records.

Multiple independently versioned cases/papers can supply evidence to one identity. Each preserves conditions, original experiment and independence identifiers, stance, strength, source anchor and author origin. Editing creates a new case version requiring new mapping and scientific review. Original `draft` confirmation flags remain unchanged. The separate accepted review means eligible for reviewed aggregation, not confirmed scientific truth or a stronger evidence class.

Source location corrections append an anchor tied to a stored Drive receipt and its exact hash; physical/printed pages and verbatim passage are retained. Human source attestation records actor, time, reason, receipt hash and passage hash. It never sets automated anchor verification. Withdrawal or a superseding anchor invalidates dependent acceptance for aggregation until corrected evidence is reviewed again. Scientific review separately records accepted, needs_revision or withdrawn, and acceptance pins the then-current source attestation. No raw JSON is required for these forms or routine case edits.

The graph checks each eligible evidence record's experiment-stage conditions before computing line style. Matched direct/strong evidence supports solid only within its claim scope; proposed/adopted/speculative alone are dashed. No matching evidence hides the connection. Refutations and unresolved independence remain visible; net-conversion observation cannot prove an elementary mechanism. Filtered history remains accessible even when connections disappear. The visible filter in this slice is temperature upper bound plus experiment stage; broader condition widgets remain future work.

Narrow RPCs derive actor and receipt hashes, enforce expected revisions and explicit attestation, and run under a constrained NOLOGIN writer. Existing tables and scientific history are retained. Apply only after hosted review and explicit authorization; respect the hosted migration mapping.
